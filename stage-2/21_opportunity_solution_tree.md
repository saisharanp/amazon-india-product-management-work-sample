# Stage 2 — Opportunity Solution Tree

**Desired outcome:** Increase trust and repeat use after Amazon India order exceptions.  
**Status:** Awaiting approval

## Outcome tree

Desired outcome  
└── Customers recover confidence after an order exception  
    ├── Opportunity 1: Understand what happened  
    │   ├── Solution: Unified order/support event timeline  
    │   ├── Solution: Confidence-based delivery states  
    │   └── Solution: Evidence summary for handoff or non-receipt  
    ├── Opportunity 2: Know who owns the issue  
    │   ├── Solution: Single case owner or queue  
    │   ├── Solution: Visible next-update SLA  
    │   └── Solution: Escalation rule for high-severity cases  
    ├── Opportunity 3: Predict the recovery outcome  
    │   ├── Solution: Refund amount and deduction explanation

    ├── Opportunity 4: Prevent avoidable exceptions  
    │   ├── Solution: Quantity and deadline confirmation  
    │   ├── Solution: Delivery promise confidence  
    │   └── Solution: Proactive exception notification  
    └── Opportunity 5: Reduce discovery uncertainty  
        ├── Solution: Verified sort/filter state  
        ├── Solution: Better comparison controls  
        └── Solution: Review and seller confidence signals

## Opportunity definitions

| Opportunity | Customer need | Evidence signal | Potential outcome metric |
|---|---|---|---|
| Understand what happened | Reconcile app state with reality | False delivery, missing order, unclear tracking | Status comprehension; repeat-contact rate |
| Know who owns the issue | Avoid repeating the story | Conflicting answers and unavailable support | First-contact resolution; transfers per case |
| Predict recovery | Know amount, date, and next action | Refund delay, deductions, inconsistent return advice | Resolution time; refund-related contacts |
| Prevent avoidable exceptions | Avoid deadline or quantity failure | Bulk cancellation and missed promise narratives | Exception rate; cancellation rate |
| Reduce discovery uncertainty | Trust browsing controls | Sort/filter reliability complaints | Search-to-detail conversion; task success |

## Recommended solution direction for Stage 3

Focus on a **unified post-purchase exception experience** composed of:

1. One customer-visible event timeline.
2. One accountable case owner or queue.
3. One explicit next action and deadline.
4. A clear recovery choice: delivery recovery, replacement, or refund.
5. A transparent refund/replacement status when selected.

Keep delivery prevention and discovery controls as later or adjacent workstreams unless internal data shows they are the dominant driver.

## Assumption map

| Assumption | Type | How to test |
|---|---|---|
| Customers prefer a clear next action over more status detail | Desirability | Concept test and usability study |
| A shared timeline reduces repeat contacts | Behavioral | Controlled rollout and contact-rate comparison |
| Support agents can access the same event state | Feasibility | Service blueprint and technical discovery |
| Refund clarity improves trust even when settlement time is unchanged | Value | Prototype test and post-resolution survey |
| High-severity cases can be reliably detected | Operational | Historical incident analysis |
